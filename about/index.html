01 — WHO WE ARE

SNACKING THE DESI WAY.

Desi Thadaka is an Indian snack brand created around the flavours people know, love and grew up enjoying.

From potato chips and peanuts to crunchy moong dal and desi mixtures, our goal is simple — create tasty snacks that bring a little more flavour to everyday life.

02 — OUR MISSION

BIG FLAVOUR. REAL DESI.

SNACKS THAT FEEL LIKE HOME.

Our mission is to create snacks that celebrate the bold and colourful flavours of India while keeping them affordable for everyday snacking.

We want Desi Thadaka to become a brand people can pick up anywhere — from a small shop to a supermarket — whenever they want something tasty and crunchy.
